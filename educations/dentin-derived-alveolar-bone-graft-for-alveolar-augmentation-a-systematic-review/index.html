Dentin-Derived Alveolar Bone Graft for Alveolar Augmentation: A Systematic Review

Publication · Systematic review

Journal: Journal of Oral Biology and Craniofacial Research, Vol. 14, 2024, pp. 395–406
DOI: 10.1016/j.jobcr.2024.05.005

Publisher ↗Request the full text

Summary

A systematic review of clinical studies in which dentin-derived grafts were used for alveolar ridge augmentation and socket preservation. It brings together the reported bone gain, graft integration and complications and comments on the quality of the available evidence.

Short summary in our own words. The full text and the authors’ abstract are on the publisher’s site.
