Progress in Dentin-Derived Bone Graft Materials: A New Xenogeneic Dentin-Derived Material with Retained Organic Component Allows for Broader and Easier Application

Publication · Review

Journal: Cells, Vol. 13, 2024, Article 1806
DOI: 10.3390/cells13211806
Licence: CC BY 4.0

Read on sitePublisher ↗

Summary

A review of dentin-derived bone graft materials: how the mineral and organic composition of dentin relates to bone, how autologous dentin grafts are prepared, and where chairside processing has practical limits.

The authors then describe a xenogeneic dentin-derived material that keeps the organic component of dentin and is supplied ready to use — the property that, according to the authors, allows broader and easier clinical application.

Short summary in our own words. The full text and the authors’ abstract are on the publisher’s site.
