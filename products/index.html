Materials in Dental Bone Reconstruction

Currently a wide range of materials are used in Dental bone reconstruction procedures:

  • Autograft: Disadvantages – very limited amount, the patient has to go through two procedures (painful bone grafting).
  • Allograft: Origin mainly from corpses; Disadvantages – expensive, limited amount of material, risk of human-to-human disease.
  • Xenograft: Origin mainly bovine and porcine; Opportunity – ‘Off the shelf’ ready to use product, most common and favorable in the market. Commercially available xenografts are mostly osteo-conductive i.e. provide a scaffold for bone formation. However, most of them are not osteogenic and neither osteo-inductive like Ivory Dentin Graft due to processing which usually eliminates all the active proteins molecules.
  • Synthetic: variety of materials, mainly hydroxyapatite Disadvantage – non organic material.

Advantages of Ivory Dentin Graft vs. Bone derived material:

  • Based upon scientific research and peer-reviewed publications on the advantages of autologous dentin grafts The first and only dentin biological graft that contains an active organic matrix, produced by Ivory Graft’s patented, unique process
  • Has the physical and mechanical properties matching the host bone
  • Ankyloses – exceptionally strong fusion with the host jaw bone, enabling volume for prosthodontic rehabilitation
  • Very slow resorption (several years) maintaining the surgical site’s 4 dimension structure
  • Powerful osteogenic properties
  • Hard nature of dentin reduces vertical compaction and maintains graft volume levels

Ivory Dentin Graft™ Perio

The Perio variant consists of granules sized 300–500 µm and is intended specifically for small and confined defects, such as those encountered in periodontitis and peri-implantitis, where complete filling with larger particles may be challenging.

The smaller granule size facilitates dense packing and intimate adaptation in confined periodontal and peri-implant defects, supporting effective defect filling and ensuring compatibility with barrier membranes in regenerative procedures.

The Perio variant is supplied sterile in glass vials of 0.25 g and 0.50 g.

The Perio variant is not intended for use in large-volume defects or for augmentation or reconstructive treatment of the alveolar ridge.

A Bone Graft Material Closest to Nature

Taking both advantages of `Dentin structure’ and ‘organic material’ Ivory Graft Ltd. has developed a xenograft tooth-derived product – the Ivory Dentin Graft – in order to expand the use of dentin-derived material for patients where autogenic tooth material is not available or suitable.

Ivory Dentin Graft™ is a porcine-derived product with both inorganic and organic non-cellular components

Bioresorbable bone graft material: In its structure Ivory Dentin Graft™ is physically and chemically similar to the mineralized matrix of human bone

Functions: Filling defects, serving as a scaffold, and hosting bone-creating cells.

Applications: Dental implants, teeth removal (ridge preservation), sinus lifting, jawbone defects.
