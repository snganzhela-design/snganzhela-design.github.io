24:T1fc3,<p>This Privacy Policy explains how Ivory Graft Ltd processes personal data when you visit ivorygraft.com and when you contact us through the website. It applies to the English and Russian versions of the website.</p>
<p>Policy version: <strong>2026-10-26-draft</strong>. The version in force at the moment you submit the contact form is recorded together with your consent.</p>
<h2 id="topic-1">Who we are</h2>
<p>The controller of your personal data is <strong>Ivory Graft Ltd</strong>, 21 Tuval St., Diamond Tower, 13th Floor - #1380, Ramat Gan 5252236, Israel. E-mail: <a href="mailto:info@ivorygraft.com">info@ivorygraft.com</a>.</p>
<p>The website is intended for healthcare professionals — dentists, oral surgeons and their teams — and for companies interested in distribution. It is not directed at children.</p>
<h2 id="topic-2">What data we collect and why</h2>
<p>We collect only the data described below. We do not sell personal data, do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling, and do not run a newsletter or user accounts.</p>
<h3>Contact form</h3>
<p>When you send a request through the <a href="/contact-us/">contact form</a>, we receive:</p>
<ul>
<li>the purpose of your request (sample, distribution partnership, clinical question, document, other);</li>
<li>your full name;</li>
<li>your phone number (optional);</li>
<li>your work e-mail address;</li>
<li>your clinic or company (optional);</li>
<li>your message;</li>
<li>a consent record: the fact that you ticked the consent box, the time of submission (UTC), the version of this policy and the language of the page.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Purpose:</strong> to answer your request — for example, to arrange a sample, discuss a partnership or answer a clinical question.</p>
<p><strong>Legal basis (GDPR):</strong> your consent (Article 6(1)(a)); where your request concerns a supply or partnership, steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)). We keep the consent record to be able to demonstrate your consent (Article 7(1)).</p>
<p>The form data is sent by e-mail to the company mailbox. The website itself does not store it in a database or in files.</p>
<h3>Technical data and security</h3>
<p>To deliver the website and protect it against abuse, the web server processes technical data: IP address, date and time of the request, requested address, browser type and the response code. These logs are kept for <strong>[retention period — to be set by the company]</strong>.</p>
<p>The contact form handler records only the fact of a submission and its status code — never the content of the form. To limit the number of requests from one address (five requests in ten minutes), it keeps a hashed form of the IP address for up to <strong>ten minutes</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Legal basis (GDPR):</strong> our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)).</p>
<h3>Analytics cookies — only with your consent</h3>
<p>The website can use Yandex Metrica and Google Analytics 4 to understand how the website is used. These counters are switched off unless the company enables them. When they are enabled, a consent banner is shown and the counters load <strong>only after you click &quot;Accept&quot;</strong>. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no requests are sent to these services.</p>
<p>You can change your choice at any time with the <strong>&quot;Cookie settings&quot;</strong> button in the footer of any page. After you withdraw consent, the page reloads without the counters.</p>
<p><strong>Legal basis (GDPR):</strong> your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). The providers are Yandex LLC (Russia) and Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC (USA); their own privacy policies apply to the data they receive.</p>
<h3>Data stored only in your browser</h3>
<p>The website stores two small entries in your browser&#39;s local storage:</p>
<ul>
<li>your choice in the analytics consent banner;</li>
<li>on the Russian version, your confirmation &quot;I am a healthcare professional&quot;, which shows clinical photos in case reports.</li>
</ul>
<p>These entries stay on your device and are not sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.</p>
<h3>What we do not use</h3>
<p>The website does not load external fonts, scripts or content delivery networks. Links to our social media pages are plain links: if you follow them, the privacy policy of the respective platform applies.</p>
<h2 id="topic-3">Who receives your data</h2>
<p>Your request is read by the Ivory Graft team members responsible for it. We use service providers acting on our behalf: the hosting provider of the website server and the e-mail provider of the company mailbox — <strong>[names and countries — to be specified by the company]</strong>. They process data only on our instructions.</p>
<p>If analytics is enabled with your consent, Yandex and Google receive the analytics data described above.</p>
<h2 id="topic-4">International transfers</h2>
<p>Ivory Graft Ltd is located in Israel. The European Commission has recognised Israel as providing an adequate level of data protection, so data from the European Union can be transferred to us without additional safeguards. Where data is transferred to other countries (for example, to analytics providers), we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.</p>
<h2 id="topic-5">How long we keep data</h2>
<ul>
<li>Contact requests and the related correspondence: <strong>[retention period — to be set by the company]</strong>, or less if you ask us to delete them.</li>
<li>Consent records: for as long as we need to demonstrate your consent, but no longer than <strong>[retention period — to be set by the company]</strong>.</li>
<li>Hashed IP address for request limiting: up to ten minutes.</li>
<li>Server logs: <strong>[retention period — to be set by the company]</strong>.</li>
<li>Analytics data: according to the settings of the analytics services, if enabled.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="topic-6">How we protect data</h2>
<p>The website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to the company mailbox and to the server is restricted to authorised persons. We apply the security measures required by the Israeli Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017, and appropriate technical and organisational measures under Article 32 GDPR.</p>
