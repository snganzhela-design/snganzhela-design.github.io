Our Product

Ivory Dentin Graft™ is available in two variants: Standard, with granules of 300–900 µm, for a broad range of bone augmentation procedures; and Perio, with fine granules of 300–500 µm, for small and confined defects such as those encountered in periodontitis and peri-implantitis.

About Ivory Dentin Graft™

A clinically proven dentin-based solution — natural, biocompatible, and designed for safe bone regeneration.

Ivory Graft

Ivory Graft Ltd. has developed Ivory Dentin Graft™ in order to expand the use of dentin‐derived material to patients where autogenic tooth material is not available

Ivory Dentin Graft™ is powered by advantages of unique

Bioresorbable Dentin Graft

In its structure, Ivory DentinGraft™ is chemically the same as human bone. In addition to mineral phase it contains full active organic matrix

Ivory Dentin Graft is CE Marked (MDR 2017/745)

Intended Use

Ivory Dentin Graft™ is intended for the repair or augmentation of bone defects in dental procedures.

Ivory Dentin Graft is intended for use in a clinical environment and may only be used by dentists or oral surgeons
